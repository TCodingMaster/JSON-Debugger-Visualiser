Rules (long; shorts mirror)

  1. A swing high is confirmed 3 bars after it prints.
  2. Break of structure: a candle closes above the last swing high.
  3. The earliest bullish FVG (gap between candle 1's high and candle 3's low, at least 0.15 × ATR) from 3 bars before to 3 bars after the break.
  4. Limit order at the top edge of the gap, valid 20 bars. Cancelled if a candle closes below the gap.
  5. Stop 2 ticks below the lowest low of the move. Target 2R.
  6. New setups only 20:00–24:00 and 02:00–05:00 New York time. Max 2 trades per day, flat by 15:55.

How it was chosen

  1. 80 rule variations were scored on the first 60% of each dataset only.
  2. The pick was the most consistent one (profitable on 10 of 10 markets × timeframes), not the highest number. The highest-scoring variant won 58–70% in that period and fell to 23–39% afterwards.
  3. The last 40% was untouched until the rules were locked.
